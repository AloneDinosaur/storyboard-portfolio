ABOUT ME

THE ARTIST BEHIND THE STORIES

HELLO, I'M CAMERON.

I am a passionate animator & storyboard artist. He is currently pursuing a degree in Visual Communication Design at Purdue University Northwest. I’m someone who believes in the power of storytelling. From the child who wants to be like their favorite character to a short film that has the right message, for the right person, at the right time. Also, I love creating cool characters, epic creatures & the exploration of “what if ...”. I’m drawn to storyboarding because I love how simple drawing can become awesome moments on screen. What attracted me to the animation industry was seeing YouTubers like Olan Rogers create their own shows. I saw how indie animation took creators with small, but loyal fanbases, to rock-stars in the animation industry. That inspired me; I want to be a part of that.
